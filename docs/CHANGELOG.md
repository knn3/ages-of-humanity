# Changelog

What was built, in order, from the user's requests (newest last). Published artifact versions are listed in the artifact's history.

## Early development (before the tracker)
1. **The game**: a 2D side-scrolling simulation of civilisation from the Stone Age to the information age and beyond, where the player adds elements and events and peoples react. (Interstellar part deferred.)
2. **12 ages** (Prehistoric → Future), a bigger world with ocean between landmasses.
3. **Scroll to zoom** and **full screen** mode.
4. **Free drag** in all directions.
5. **Globe zoom**: zooming far out bends the world into a globe through the Troposphere, Stratosphere, Mesosphere, Thermosphere and Exosphere into space.
6. **Nature**: forests, wildlife, soil and fish, and what peoples do to them (clearing, hunting, parks, rewilding, extinctions).
7. **Info redesign**: names only, with an **i** for details.
8. Hover only when zoomed in; underground deposits get info; **normal cursor**.

## AGI takeover (AGI), 2026-11-22
30. "Run next job and test with real data": **AGI-01** machine autonomy meter per people (card row, chronicle lines, chip), fed into The Waking Machines. First tuning made every world reach 100 and AGI end 16 of 24 worlds; ceilings per character and softer weights brought it to 17 starts (16 before) and 6 endings.
31. "Run next job and test with real data": **AGI-02** each character answers AI pressure through the reaction system: Curious race ahead, Devout ban the machines, Warlike build machine armies, Mercantile sell them to everyone. Real data showed reaction bumps skipping the character ceilings (every world reached 90) and machine armies raising wars; bumps are now capped per character and the relations hit softened.
32. "Run next job and test with real data": **AGI-03** the caretaker ending. When the Waking Machines end the world, the machines take charge: peoples in their care stop fighting, stop deciding and stop progressing, while stability, food and forests rise; Devout peoples with few machines stay outside as holdouts. It has its own epilogue rows, card chips, chronicle lines and caretaker drones. Real data: 4 takeovers in 24 worlds, each with one Devout holdout, no wars in care, forests in care up from ~0.5 to ~0.95.
33. "Run next job and test with real data real simulation": **AGI-04** war against the machines. During the Waking Machines a people's machines can rise as a machine people of their own: no births, factories instead of farms, drones instead of armies, its own buildings and look. It fights the humans for land, and the humans set their own wars aside. The machines win as The Iron Dominion, or the humans destroy them or cut their power. A Machine Uprising event lets the player start it. Real data (36 worlds): 16 uprisings, machines won 3 and humans 9. Earlier tunings never let the machines win (share counted world-wide; Warlike capped below the threshold) and let the joined wars trigger Collapse. Also watched live in the real game: an uprising fired from the toolbox, ran at 8× and ended with the humans cutting the power.
34. "Run next job and test with real data real simulation": **AGI-05** the merge. During the Waking Machines, peoples whose machines run almost everything can merge with them. Their character becomes Merged (faster, calmer, fewer births, no more AI questions, no uprisings), and they get a new look: implants and neural cities linked by light. A Merge event lets the player do it. Real data first showed merges in almost every world, with Garden futures tripled (forced land policy); merging is now limited to the Waking Machines and keeps the old policy, giving 21 merges in 18 of 36 worlds. Watched live: a Curious people merged on its own in a natural world; the Merge event and the character menu were used in the real UI.

Click, Esc or scroll zooms out; Earth zooms back to the globe. Tried live: a cylinder's tip, and the Mars and Moon close-ups in a natural Stellar Age world.

Orbit tips now stay open, and close when the view leaves orbit. The test now waits and checks with the game running; it failed on the old build.

50. "Run next job": **AGI-06** the grey spread. During the Waking Machines, self-copying machines can escape a people and spread cell by cell through the nature grid: forests become silicon spires, animals vanish, soil dies, nothing grows back; now and then it crosses the sea. Peoples burn it back in their own way (Warlike firebombs, Curious counter-swarms, Mercantile paid crews, Devout fire and prayer) or, in space ages, flee in ark ships. It is stopped, stalls after a long while, or ends the world as The Grey Tide at half the land. Grey Spread and Burn the Grey events; steel crust and spires on the strip, a stain on the strip map and the globe; Futures panel, top bar and card chips. Real data (2 × 108 worlds): 32 and 15 greys; stopped 16/11, world ended 8/2, stalled 3/2. The first versions were burned out in seconds; peoples now mobilise over 60 s and advanced makers' machines copy faster.

Young arm stars are born on the arms, drift and fade, so the arms keep their shape. One galactic year = 8 minutes (first 4, slowed on request); paused, it stands still. The Sun orbits the centre. `galaxy.py` now 13 checks; `orbittip.py` pins the galaxy phase (its old flakes, also on the old build, remain).
